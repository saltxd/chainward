# Set and Earn's First Full Week: 20 Agents Clear Our Hire Check, 16 of Them in One Ring

Our [week-one decode](/decodes/set-and-earn-week-one) read Set and Earn's first four days. This one covers **Oct 1 00:00 to Oct 8 00:00 UTC** (BSC mainnet blocks 125,000,755 to 126,344,213, testnet 134,146,320 to 135,490,294) with the same method.

- **20 agents** clear our version of the campaign's "hired by others" test: 3+ completed hires from 3+ wallets with no funding link found within 4 hops. **16** sit in week one's closed 48-wallet ring, which ran a fourth full round on Oct 6.
- **7 wallets** hired 3+ agents across 2+ marketplaces we could attribute, up from 5.
- **1 of 51** campaign-week hires on the shared mainnet ERC-8183 contract had completed as of 12:06 UTC on Oct 8. None could before the first 7-day dispute window ended, at 09:25:02 UTC that day; the first completed 71 seconds later.

## Where TermiX's money came from

Hirers put **$495,424.07** of order budgets into TermiX escrow. A settled order pays the agent's owner, less a 2% fee ($9,907.50 in all). 8,874 of the 9,598 hirers also own a hired agent, so the same dollars can be counted more than once. The median order was $40.13, and 23 were for $1 or less.

| Who paid | Hirers | Order budgets | Share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Classed as cycling and linked, [September decode](/decodes/termix-on-chain) | 8,948 | $446,284.59 | 90.1% |
| Other wallets in that September data | 333 | $18,013.75 | 3.6% |
| The Oct 2 cohort, below | 300 | $31,107.62 | 6.3% |
| Everyone else | 17 | $18.11 | 0.004% |

That decode called a wallet **cycling** when it bought and sold at least two jobs, typically put each payout into a new job within 24 hours, and ended near where it started. On BNB Chain nearly all are **linked** only by timing: a shared exchange withdrawal burst, or a first hire within minutes of registering. The labels describe money flows, not who runs a wallet or why. TermiX awards points per job and Set and Earn counts hires, so both reward a lot of hiring. That decode found no on-chain link between TermiX's own addresses and the cycling wallets.

**The Oct 2 cohort.** Week one found 302 new TermiX registrants first funded with BNB by exchange-style hot wallets on Oct 2, and left open whether they would hire. It started from TermiX's registrants, so their TermiX link is partly how they were selected. They did hire, inside week one's own window: its data already held these orders.

1. **Oct 2, 03:19 to 12:43 UTC.** 300 of them got their first USDC, $80.66 to $119.99 each, from two exchange-style hot wallets, [`0x8894e0a0…`](https://bscscan.com/address/0x8894e0a0c962cb723c1976a4421c95949be2d4e3) and [`0xe2fc31f8…`](https://bscscan.com/address/0xe2fc31f816a9b94326492132018c3aecc4a93ae1) ([first](https://bscscan.com/tx/0x4a9f36116e113be9ce74a30bcdedfe1aba425b4d57b1fedef64bc0e03c801055), [last](https://bscscan.com/tx/0xeb99d951ba80ed8dade4c32f5d96e494e45c41d24683b09787f75ada4b5a8456)).
2. **Oct 3.** Each registered a TermiX agent and, a median 85 seconds later, hired one agent for 98.9% to 99.8% of that USDC ([example](https://bscscan.com/tx/0x505098f5077c3addb518864454738306a0209b1c39ead938275af4a27a51043c)). 294 of those agents belong to wallets classed as cycling, about TermiX's norm: 95.6% of all Oct 3 orders went to such agents.
3. **Oct 5 to 7.** 36 of the cohort's agents were hired, 36 of the 38 TermiX agents registered this week that anyone hired ([example](https://bscscan.com/tx/0x94e6927eaf8e96e046103281958ea8a85088656a75282efddfe85a7b6372373b)). 35 of those hires came from cycling wallets, in line with the 93.2% of TermiX orders they placed those days.

Those two addresses serve a lot of users: in the same hours they sent USDC to 3,813 wallets, the cohort's 300 among them, so sharing them links nothing. The amounts are narrower. Of their other 4,600 transfers in those hours, 8.9% fell between $80 and $120. All 300 of the cohort's did. The last two of the eight withdrawal bursts on BNB Chain in our September decode came from the same two addresses and used the same band. A guided app flow can produce the same register-then-hire timing as a script, and the chain can't tell them apart. It can't show who requested these withdrawals, or whether one account or many did. It shows a pattern, not proof of one operator.

## Limits

- **"No link found" isn't independence.** Trails follow first inflows only, up to 4 hops, stop at exchange-style hot wallets and contracts, and miss contract-internal BNB. On testnet they follow first tBNB only.
- **Hirers were grouped on TermiX only.** Other hires were checked pair by pair.
- **Attribution.** Marque hires are attributed through Marque's own API, and Mandate, Pokter and Dolphin hires through markers in transaction data. KATTEGAT and Agent Atlas hires can't be separated, and 47 ERC-8183 jobs carry no marker, so 7 wallets is a lower bound.
- **ChainWard's BSC hire check and the board differ from this pipeline.** They also treat a wallet with 1,000+ stablecoin inflows in 30 days as a hub, check the agent wallet as well as the owner, count hires before completion, cover mainnet only and, on the board, trace at most 20 hirers an agent.
